🔐 Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 April 2026 · NoveaTech Lab (Harare, ZW + SA shipping)

We collect only what's needed to repair your device, build your web solution, or provide IT support. No selling, no shady data brokering.

1. What we collect

  • Device data: model, serial, repair history, diagnostic logs (only with consent).
  • Contact info: name, email, phone, shipping address for kits/repairs.
  • Web/IT projects: credentials you provide for hosting, domain, or CMS access.
  • Analytics: anonymized usage on our site (no personal tracking).

2. How we use it

  • Repair diagnostics, warranty fulfilment, and parts ordering.
  • Web development: deploy, test, and maintain your sites/apps.
  • Service updates, booking reminders, and tech tips (opt-out anytime).
  • Improve our tools and teardown guides.

3. Data retention

  • Repair records: 3 years (for warranty & parts traceability).
  • Web project data: retained for the duration of your maintenance contract.
  • You can request deletion anytime — we'll wipe it within 30 days.

4. Third-party sharing

  • We never sell data. We share only with shipping carriers (address) and Apple/authorized parts suppliers (serial for warranty).
  • For web hosting, we use reputable providers (AWS, Hetzner) — data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

5. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data — email privacy@noveatech.zw.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.
